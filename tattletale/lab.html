Plate bake-off

Runs the phone's plate reader and the AI models on public US plate photos (OpenALPR's end-to-end benchmark, loaded from GitHub; mostly California plates) and scores each against the true plate. The AI models use the key or server saved in the app's About page. A run of 50 photos costs a few cents.
