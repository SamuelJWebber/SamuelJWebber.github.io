Dashboards

Research dashboards built from public data. Each one says where its numbers come from.

US credit card data
How much Americans owe on cards, what they pay and who falls behind. Rebuilt nightly from 14 public sources.
Market Calibration Atlas
How well prediction-market prices match what actually happened, across venues, events and horizons.
NYC Street Flow
What replacing a traffic lane with a protected bike lane does to how many people move, and how fast.
Citi Bike, day by day
Every day of NYC bike-share rides since 2013, with price changes and closures on the timeline. Refreshed monthly.
Senate Coattails
Does a stronger Senate nominee lift the rest of the ticket? Tested through the 2026 midterms.
Consumer credit risk from ABS filings
Card trusts and auto-loan securitisations, read from SEC filings.
Winter 2026-27 ski research
Where the snow is likeliest and what a refundable week costs, with every price dated.
TattleTale NYC
Snap a car blocking a bike lane, hydrant or crosswalk and it fills in the 311 complaint, then checks whether NYPD really showed up.
