Which clock, which sample,
which baseline?

Five checks on earlier results: when the answer became public compared with when trading closed, what that does to the dashboard's election scores, whether the overreaction seen in March holds in a month nobody had looked at and then in a later period on another exchange, and whether consumer-credit markets beat a forecast built only from published history.

Specifications for studies 08–10 were committed before their test data were fetched or scored, and for study 11 before any re-anchored score was computed. Code and frozen inputs are in studies/ in the source repository.

These results are descriptive. None is a causal estimate or a trading return. Intervals are approximate 95% Student-t intervals clustered by parent event unless stated; lower Brier is better.
