USING THE SIGNAL WITHOUT CHANGING ITS MEANING

A practical research agenda for lenders

Use these markets as candidate inputs to a portfolio forecast. Their value is the improvement they deliver on future, fully observed lending cohorts.

01

Separate delinquency from loss

A late account, an annualized charge-off rate and principal unrecovered after 60 days are different targets. Match the contract’s definition to the decision before comparing forecasts.

02

Match the population

National bank-card and mortgage statistics do not isolate low-FICO borrowers or cash advances. Test their incremental signal on the relevant product, vintage and risk segment.

03

Use only what was known then

Align prices to their actual timestamps and distinguish the performance quarter from the publication date. A market opened after a quarter ends is a nowcast of its release, not a forecast made before that quarter.

04

Compare with an existing forecast

Evaluate the baseline model against the same model plus market information on the same future cohorts. Include public economic data, shifts in underwriting and portfolio mix in the comparison.

Scope of this page: these are public consumer-finance outcomes. This collection does not establish a forecast of a particular lender’s losses, cash-advance repayment, or the legal effect of an EWA rule. Spending indices are not credit-loss rates.
