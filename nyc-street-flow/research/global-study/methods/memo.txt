MULTI-CITY CAUSAL METHODS AUDIT
Prepared November 5, 2026

Finding
Expanding cities improves the opportunity to measure a real lane conversion and construct comparisons. It does not allow cycling-only, vehicle-only and unrelated city transit totals to be added into a causal all-person result. No paper acquired by this methods workstream identifies the joint effect on network person-throughput and complete journey time. This is a result of the screen, not a claim that no such study exists anywhere.

Concrete work completed
The Kraus/Koch Zenodo archive was downloaded and independently checked against its published checksum. It contains a Git-LFS pointer instead of the advertised 330 MB analysis panel. The linked repository and object were unavailable. The archive predates the final paper. Consequently no replication estimate is claimed; reproduce_access_audit.py reproduces the access/byte/checksum audit offline, not an econometric result. See replication-access-audit.json.

The key direct-conversion case remains Berlin. Use the separate Europe acquisition/audit for official raw data. The paper’s across-lane mean volume and documented detector deinstallations make cross-section reconstruction necessary before inferring that total cars barely changed. Do not convert its published motor spot-speed result into complete commute minutes.

The additional paper register keeps mechanisms separate: Montréal illustrates survey mode substitution, Oslo route diversion, Atlanta app-calibrated distance and Victoria null/contaminated comparisons. The new 2026 NYC paper provides a current matched-DiD bikeshare comparator but does not supply the missing all-mode outcome. Abstract-only papers are flagged and are not ready for quantitative pooling.

How to strengthen the dashboard now
1. Show eligibility, measurement and causal-design gates independently. A verified lane removal with aligned multimode counts may be strong measurement but weak identification.
2. Keep city-specific results visible and label author-reported, independently recalculated and causally estimated values distinctly.
3. Register all found conversions, including reversals and data failures. Never choose cities based on positive cycling results.
4. Use transportability-protocol.txt before estimating any pooled result. It defines independent units, matching outcomes, interference, mediator avoidance, NYC effect modifiers and support checks.
5. If the strongest eligible case still lacks passenger occupancy, report explicit bounds or a partial outcome. Do not fill the gap with citywide average occupancy and present it as observed person-flow.
