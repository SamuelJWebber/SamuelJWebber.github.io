NYC STREET FLOW — INDEPENDENT REVIEW OF THE DASHBOARD EXPANSION
Review date: November 5, 2026

RESULT
The released arithmetic audited here reconciles. No reviewed calculation
establishes a causal all-mode effect of a bike-lane conversion. That scientific
limit remains visible in the dashboard; arithmetic validation is not causal
validation. Browser and visual checks are documented separately by the site owner.

INDEPENDENT CHECKS
1. Recomputed 1,008 McGuinness directional and hourly estimates from 5,404
   released source direction-day rows. The separate pandas implementation
   reconstructs common dates, quality exclusions, matching and equal cell weights.
   Nine numeric fields per comparison reconcile; maximum absolute discrepancy
   is 1.46e-11, consistent with floating-point summation.
2. Recomputed the PPW survey distributions from raw observations using NumPy:
   64 per-day groups and 32 pooled-wave groups. Counts, means, medians, quartiles,
   P90, minima and maxima reconcile. These are descriptive survey quantiles,
   not confidence intervals or estimates of long-run commute reliability.
3. Checked all 20 possible mode-switch directions, 500 reproducibly generated
   weighted cohorts, no-change and invalid-input cases. The journey calculator
   conserves travelers and reconciles weighted totals and means without counting
   switchers twice. It is a hypothetical fixed-cohort calculation, not throughput.
4. Checked PPW passenger and occupancy reversal identities across both periods,
   all three motor baselines and multiple occupancy and passenger assumptions.
   Default conditional ranges reconcile: AM -71.57 to +142.43 people/hour;
   PM -39.67 to +86.43 people/hour. These are neither empirical hard bounds nor
   causal confidence intervals.
5. Reviewed the geographical and editorial separation of bridge vs approach
   counter, observed segments vs complete journeys, distant donors vs local
   diversion routes, and measured inputs vs modeled totals and missing outcomes.

ISSUES CORRECTED DURING REVIEW
- The legacy PPW uniform-occupancy result is now labeled a scenario. The project
  overview uses the bus-adjusted range and links directly to its assumptions.
- Similar-looking Third Avenue time estimates now link to the longer fixed-mix
  comparison, rather than appearing as independent confirmations.
- Occupied journey groups require positive complete time. Missing/blank inputs
  cannot silently become zero travel time.
- A uniform car-time reduction that would require negative travel time is marked
  infeasible. Inactive zero-time alternatives do not crash the frontier; they are
  omitted with a reason until their hypothetical times are supplied.
- JSON scenario exports omit the CSV-oriented byte-order mark so strict JSON
  readers can parse the file.
- Reversal-calculator assumptions are preserved in shareable view parameters.
  Nonfinite results and invalid bus-run inputs are rejected.
- Pulaski timing language no longer treats a September 2015 public update as
  proof that all earlier months were free of preparatory work. The earlier
  Jan–Feb check is shown with its short and incomplete support; the broader
  earlier comparison remains a sensitivity.

UNRESOLVED SCIENTIFIC LIMITS
- McGuinness station 021284 is not a Pulaski Bridge passenger counter. Its counts
  do not measure speed, capacity, vehicle occupancy, or total network travelers.
  Permanent lane-closure timing and detector continuity still require confirmation.
- The directional donor roads are in the Bronx, not demonstrated untreated
  equivalents. Lower observed traffic may reflect demand, diversion, switching,
  suppressed journeys or detector changes. Northbound is not assumed untreated.
- PPW has two weekday survey dates per wave; the bus reroute and other street
  changes affect its comparison roads. Modal count dates and locations differ.
- Third Avenue is a bundled redesign. Its pre-period divergence and incomplete
  passenger/whole-journey evidence prevent a bike-lane-only causal claim.
- The fixed-cohort time tool deliberately holds traveler count and composition
  fixed. New or forgone trips need separate reporting; minimizing total travel
  minutes alone is not a complete welfare or accessibility objective.
- Source coverage, raw counts, model choices and scenario assumptions are distinct
  sources of uncertainty. Neither the number of rows nor the number of favorable
  specifications is a probability that a policy works.

REPRODUCE THESE CHECKS
python independent_data_audit.py
  Requires pandas and NumPy. Uses files in ../directional, ../estimate-lab and
  ../causal. Its JSON result records input hashes.
node independent_math_audit.mjs
  Requires Node.js and the released dashboard modules. Uses no browser or network.

Outputs: independent-data-audit.json; independent-math-audit.json.
These scripts independently check arithmetic rather than importing the data
producer functions. The calculator audit imports the public pure calculation
functions and checks them against separate sums and accounting identities.
