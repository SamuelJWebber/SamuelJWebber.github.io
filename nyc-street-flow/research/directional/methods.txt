McGUINNESS APPROACH: DIRECTION AND CLOCK HOUR
Analysis date: November 5, 2026

Question and boundary
The 2016 Pulaski bikeway removed a Brooklyn-bound / southbound bridge lane.
NYSDOT station 021284 / 02_1284 / CC0280 is farther south, between Meeker
Avenue / I-278 and Greenpoint Avenue. Intervening junctions prevent treating
this counter as the bridge screenline. This is a historical 2014-2016 study,
not a test of any subsequent McGuinness redesign. Counts include motor
vehicles of all types; no occupancy, people, speed or capacity is inferred.

The December 2014 plan says three southbound bridge lanes already merged
to two near McGuinness / Green Street. Removing a lane along the bridge is
not, by itself, proof that the downstream bottleneck lost a lane/capacity.

New finding
On identical common support, 2014 to 2016 observed all-day counts fell
6.38% southbound and 7.06% northbound. The PM-window decline is 5.90%
southbound and 15.44% northbound. Both directions decline; northbound is
not used as an untreated control. The pattern is not concentrated only
in the removed bridge lane's direction. These observations do not identify
the cause or exclude network effects, diversion, demand or detector changes.

weekday cells, 70 baseline dates and 66 after dates. Each cell has at least

hours and directions share exactly the same dates and cell weights.

Counterfactual diagnostic
For each direction and time window, scale the 2014 McGuinness mean by
comparison-year / 2014 growth at a donor. Donors are Boston Road 010009
and Broadway 010012, both in the Bronx. Donor outcomes are bidirectional
in the identical clock-hour window. Formula:
  expected_target = target_2014 * donor_later / donor_2014
  relative_change_pct = 100 * (target_later / expected_target - 1)
Means give each retained month by weekday cell equal weight. Within a
cell, dates receive equal weight. Missing cells are not imputed; equal
retained-cell weights do not imply equal month weights. These are
sensitivity comparisons, not identified causal effects or confidence bounds.

Timing and earlier-period diagnostics
Construction was documented by September 22, 2015, when DOT said it had
recently started. The bikeway opened April 29, 2016. The December 2014
plan scheduled an April 2015 project start, temporary daytime and nighttime
lane closures, and noted a November 3, 2014 notice to proceed. A schedule
does not establish actual closures. Exact permanent motor-lane closure
and any preparatory restrictions remain unverified.

The preferred conservative earlier comparison is January-February 2014
versus January-February 2015. Only 5/10 requested cells survive; this is
too short to establish seasonal parallel trends or guarantee no anticipation.
The broader January-August 2014/2015 diagnostic retains 21/40 common cells
across January-June and may overlap preparatory work. It is not a verified
untreated pre-period. Earlier-period diagnostics are not held-out forecasts.
The 2017 Kosciuszko opening is a competing network change; no 2017 effects
are presented here.

Quality and direction mapping
Both directions at every station must contain 24 present, nonnegative
hourly values. Main sensitivity excludes a common date if ANY of the six
source direction records contains an hourly zero; the alternative keeps
zeros. A zero can be real, so this is a sensitivity screen, not an agency
fault diagnosis. No interpolation or zero filling is performed.

For McGuinness, old ROAD99 is begin-to-end (I-278 / Meeker to Greenpoint),
mapped northbound; ROAD-99 is southbound. New FEDERAL_DIRECTION1 and5
map north and south. Original codes and rows are preserved. Stable station
IDs and a defensible direction mapping do not certify unchanged detector
placement or calibration across the 2016 format change.

Reproduction
Unzip reproduction.zip and run: python reproduce.py
Python 3 standard library only. Official source archives live in sources/.
The script emits analysis.json, comparisons.csv, hourly-comparisons.csv,
monthly-comparisons.csv, matched-dates.csv and the extracted raw directional
station-day CSV. It tests direction sums, hourly sums, duplicate source
keys and reconciliation with the previously audited common-support result.
The raw source CSV has 5,404 direction rows across the three stations.

Independent audit
A separately implemented pandas reconstruction checked 1,008 period/hour
rows and nine numeric fields from the released raw CSV. Maximum absolute
numeric discrepancy: 1.46e-11. The separate validation directory contains
its code and full report. Arithmetic agreement does not establish causality.

Limits
No aligned person counts or complete journey times at this boundary.
No basis for converting fewer detected vehicles into fewer people moved.
Traffic displaced elsewhere and car-to-subway shifts remain unmeasured.
Distant donors, missing months, earlier trend differences and unknown
instrument continuity prevent a signed causal conclusion.
