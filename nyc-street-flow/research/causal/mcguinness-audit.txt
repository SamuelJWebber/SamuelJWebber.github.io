Material presentation/design fixes

1. Control validation does not use identical support across controls.
   Boston's January–August 2014/2015 pre-check contains 34/40 cells and eight
   months; Broadway's contains 22/40 cells and January–June only. Its absence
   of July/August means the 11.10% and 4.54% monthly pre-RMSPE scores do not
   constitute a like-for-like control ranking. Do not label Broadway a
   validated counterfactual or select it solely by those scores.

   A separate common-three-station sensitivity now uses the same dates and
   month×weekday cells for both controls. It retains 21/40 pre-check cells,
   January–June, and 65 baseline/63 later dates. Daily RMSPE is 7.24% for
   Boston and 4.40% for Broadway. PM ranking reverses: Boston 5.50%, Broadway
   6.80%. Neither is validated by this short, nonexperimental check.

2. Window labels describe requested windows, not full observed panels.
   The primary 2014/2016 pairwise Boston comparison retains 31/40 cells:
   May–November only, with just Wednesday in May and no December. Broadway
   retains 26/40 cells: May, June, September–December, without July/August.
   These require visible retained-month/cell/date counts. A raw McGuinness
   estimate also changes between controls because its source sample changes.

   Equal month×weekday-cell weights are correctly implemented, but this is
   not equal weighting of months: a month with five retained weekdays has
   five times the weight of one with only one. Monthly pre-RMSPE instead
   weights retained months equally. Both are acceptable descriptive choices
   if their definitions and missingness are stated.

3. The 2017 control comparison is especially fragile.
   Pairwise retained cells are only 13/40 (Boston) and 11/40 (Broadway) for
   unflagged observations. On dates/cells shared by all three stations,
   only two May cells survive: four baseline and five 2017 dates. Its
   cross-control variation should not be presented as a stable full-window
   result. The Kosciuszko Bridge opening is an additional competing change.

4. Describe the estimator accurately.
   Main result = target-after mean / (target-before mean × control-after
   mean / control-before mean) − 1, using equal retained-cell weights for
   each of the four means. It is a ratio of means, not an equal-weight mean
   of cell-specific relative effects. The code comment saying ratios are
   averaged is inaccurate; its output formula is correct. Cell RMSE/median
   use cell-specific ratios and are separate diagnostics.

5. Flagging and completeness are availability screens, not fault diagnoses.
   Zero values are retained in source totals and trigger possible_dropout.
   Their exclusion is a sensitivity choice; do not label flagged rows proven
   detector failures or remaining rows guaranteed accurate. Peak all_complete
   results require only the selected three hours in each direction, not all
   24 hours. The unflagged branch does require no missing/zero hourly values
   anywhere that day. Label quality branches with those definitions.

target sample. No causal confidence interval or all-person conversion is
justified by the acquired data.

Checks passed

An independent standard-library reconstruction matches all 36 comparison
rows; largest absolute numeric difference is 2.18e−11. The recorded input
SHA256 matches the acquired CSV. Within each pair/year, target and control
share exact dates. Corresponding years share month and weekday labels,
not the same dates of the month or holidays. At least two paired source
dates per retained cell is enforced, and September 2015–April 2016 does
not enter the reported pre/post comparisons. Outcomes are vehicle counts,
not passengers, trip completion, capacity, or travel time.

Independent source QA confirms all 15,907 nonblank full-day totals contain
two opposite direction records with 24 present, nonnegative values each;
all sums reconcile. No duplicate station/date/source-direction records
exist in the current 37,009 directional rows. Stable station identifiers
support continuity, but the 2016 export schema drops instrument identifiers
and detailed endpoints; unchanged calibration/placement cannot be certified.
