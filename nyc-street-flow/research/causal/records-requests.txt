Subject: Existing Third Avenue E59–E96 evaluation records, 2022–2024 — first stage

To the NYC DOT Records Access Officer:

Please provide electronic copies of the following existing records dated January
1, 2022 through December 31, 2024 concerning the Third Avenue project between East
59th and East 96th Streets, Manhattan:

1. Existing evaluation plans, count-location indexes, survey inventories, data
   dictionaries and collection/quality-control documentation identifying vehicle
   classification, vehicle occupancy, bicycle, pedestrian, bus-passenger and
   travel-time observations used for this project. Include site/segment IDs,
   coordinates or cross streets, directions, dates, observation intervals and
   original units where recorded. This asks for maintained records, not a newly
   compiled inventory or an assessment of what analysis is possible.

2. Existing as-built plans, work orders, acceptance/completion logs and signal
   timing or transit-signal-priority change logs establishing actual activation,
   installation, removal or suspension dates for general traffic lanes, the bus
   lane, bicycle lane, stops, loading rules, turn restrictions, signals and signal
   priority. Include recorded temporary closures, construction phases and lane
   availability. A press-release or project-wide completion date is not a
   substitute for existing block/intersection activation records.

3. Existing survey inventories and project/change logs for these candidate
   comparison corridors, limited to the same dates: First, Lexington and Madison
   Avenues between East 59th and East 96th Streets. Prioritize count locations at
   or nearest East 60th, 72nd, 86th and 96th Streets. Include existing records of
   concurrent lane, signal, bus-priority, stop or construction changes. These
   records will be used to assess whether a comparison is suitable, not to presume
   that these corridors were untreated.

4. Any existing evaluation extracts or count sheets for May 8–14, 2023 and May
   6–12, 2024 at the identified Third Avenue sites, limited to 07:00–10:00 and
   16:00–19:00 local time. If those dates were not observed, please provide an
   existing sample file or report from the nearest documented survey campaign;
   do not generate replacement observations, estimates or new summaries.

Native CSV/XLSX/GIS files and existing dictionaries are preferred where maintained;
PDF is suitable for documents. Existing public download links are acceptable.
Do not provide license plates, faces, identifiable video, individual travel
histories or other personal identifiers. No new collection or analysis is
requested. If responsive records are not maintained, a response to that effect
is sufficient. Please provide separable responsive records where available.

No fees or chargeable processing are authorized. Please notify me of any proposed
charge and obtain separate agreement before incurring it. Electronic delivery is
preferred. This stage does not authorize the larger follow-up below.
