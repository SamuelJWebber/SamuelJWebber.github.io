New York City · Bike lane conversions

More people per hour?
Shorter trips?

What happens when a driving lane becomes a bikeway? Count the people who get through in an hour. Then measure how long it takes to get between the same two places, including stops and waits. On a fixed route, a shorter trip means a higher average speed.
